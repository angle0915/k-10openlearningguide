Understanding Modules and Course Pages

The Content Tab is where you organize your term. Here is how to construct a logical flow for basic education students:

1. Setting up your Course Modules:
Break down your subjects into manageable Modules (e.g., "Quarter 1", "Quarter 2", or "Week 1"). This keeps students from feeling overwhelmed.
2. Understanding Course Pages:
Inside each module, you will add "Pages". A page is where your course content and learning activities actually live. Think of a page as a single lesson for the day.
3. Using Page Templates & Design Patterns:
To save time and keep a consistent look for your young learners, you can use built-in page templates.

Adding Content, Resources & Recommended Widgets

Widgets are interactive blocks you can drag and drop onto your course pages. They allow you to add text, images, videos, files, and more.

For Kinder to Grade 6 (Focus on Visuals & Simple Interactions)

  • Text & Image Widgets: Use lots of colorful images. Keep text paragraphs short and font sizes large.
  • Video Widget: Embed sing-along videos, animated stories, or your own recorded mini-lectures.
  • Matching Activity Widget: Great for vocabulary, identifying shapes, or matching animals to habitats.
  • Simple Quiz Widget (Multiple Choice): Add immediate feedback for younger students so they feel rewarded.

For Grade 7 to Grade 10 (Focus on Collaboration & Critical Thinking)

  • Share / Discussion Widgets: Perfect for designing great activities where students can debate a topic and learn from each other.
  • File Submission Widget: Use this for essays, projects, and presentation slides.
  • Advanced Quizzes & Checklists: Help older students track their own progress through self-assessment widgets.

How to Add Sections and Classes

To organize your handled advisories or subject sections properly within your course settings, follow these steps:

Step-by-Step Guide:
1. Go to your course dashboard and click on Course Setup on the left side panel.
2. Select and click on Classes.
3. Click the Create New Class button to add the specific sections of the classes you handled.

How to Enroll a Student

⚠️ Note for New Teachers: You can safely skip this step for now! Our students may not all be fully registered with OpenLearning yet. You can focus entirely on building your course content first.

Once your students are registered and your course is published, here is how you will let them in:

Method 1: Inviting via Email
1. Navigate to the Administer Students tab on the left-hand menu.
2. Click on Learners.
3. Click the Invite Learners button.
4. Paste the MCU Microsoft email addresses of your students and click send.
Method 2: Using a Course Link (Recommended for Grade 7-10)
1. Go to your Course Settings.
2. Ensure your course privacy allows for student enrollment.
3. Copy the Course URL from your landing page and distribute it to your class group chats or channels.

🎯 Final Activity: Create Your Own Course!

Now it is your turn to apply what you've learned. As a final step in your onboarding, please complete the following activity:

  1. Log in: Go to MCU OpenLearning and log in.
  2. Create a Course: Click "Create a Course" and name it according to the subject you will be teaching this school year.
  3. Set Up the Landing Page: Use the syllabus that you created for this schoool year as your guide. Fill out the course summary and expected outcomes on your homepage.
  4. Build One Module: Create your "Quarter 1" module and add at least one page for your first lesson.
  5. Add Widgets: On your page, Drag and drop at least one Text Widget, one Image Widget, and one interaction widget suitable for your grade level.
  6. Post Your Outline: For your very first post/page in the module, we highly recommend you post your 1st Quarter Course Outline to set clear expectations for your students.

Note: Use your attached syllabus strictly as a reference to fill in the textual content on your landing page and module structure!
